## 🌟 Core Highlights

- **Zero Vendor Lock-In**: 100% free and open-source under CPAL 1.0. No per-server fees or paywalls.
- **HIPAA, GDPR & FINRA Compliant**: Documents never leave the user's browser sandbox.
- **High-DPI Retina Rendering**: Crisp 60 FPS pinch-zoom and 360° mouse drag panning.
